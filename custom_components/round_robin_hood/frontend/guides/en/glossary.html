Glossary

Term Meaning
PV Photovoltaic solar electricity generation.
Grid export Instantaneous power sent to grid; sensor sign is configured.
Grid import Instantaneous power purchased from grid.
W Watt, instantaneous electrical power; enter a 4 kW nameplate as 4000 W.
Wh / kWh Energy = power × time; 1000 W for one hour = 1 kWh.
kWp Nameplate PV capacity under standard test conditions; RRH displays the system rating in W.
Reserve Watts deliberately held back from allocations.
Hysteresis Return gap preventing frequent toggling near a threshold.
Allocation RRH-planned and confirmed usage of a consumer.
Fairness credit Credited RRH runtime or estimated Wh, separate from general statistics.
Strict / Skip Wait for blocked next candidate vs. consider a fitting one.
Residual-surplus load Lowest-priority adjustable device using watts left after normal profiles.
