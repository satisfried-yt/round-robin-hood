# Round Robin Hood — branding and attribution guidance

**Scope:** This is guidance on project attribution and avoiding misleading branding. It is not proof of trademark registration.

The Round Robin Hood code, documentation and distributed images are provided under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), to the extent the publisher has the relevant rights and except for separately licensed third-party material. **This policy does not add restrictions to the software's Apache-2.0 copyright license.**

## Project identity

The project's identifying names and imagery include:

- **Round Robin Hood** and **RRH**, when used to identify this Home Assistant integration.
- **Satisfried**, the project publisher's pseudonym.
- The project's mascot, icons, logo and associated distinctive visual presentation.

Apache-2.0 section 6 does **not** grant a license to use the publisher's trade names, trademarks or product names as marks, beyond customary use for describing the work's origin and reproducing the NOTICE. Rights arising from trademark, name or unfair-competition law, **if any**, exist separately from copyright permissions. This policy does not claim exclusive rights in common words, the Robin Hood story, public-domain matter or third-party branding.

## Uses we welcome

- Refer truthfully to the official Round Robin Hood software, link to the official repository, and retain original copyright and NOTICE attribution.
- Write tutorials, reviews, comparisons or documentation using the name and relevant screenshots where otherwise lawful.
- Describe a modified version accurately as **"based on Round Robin Hood"**, **"forked from Round Robin Hood"** or similar, while clearly identifying its independent maintainer.
- Show the official icon or logo to identify genuine official RRH releases, including installation and display through the Home Assistant Marketplace (and legacy HACS versions).

## Avoid misleading affiliation

Please do not use the project name, mascot or logo in ways that falsely suggest that an independent fork, product, channel, download or service is an official **Satisfried** release, sponsored by the project, or endorsed by its maintainer. Modified redistributions should use their own prominent project identity and label their relationship to RRH accurately.

This guidance concerns **brand confusion**, not whether forks may be written, distributed, sold or used commercially: all those software activities remain allowed under Apache-2.0. Accurate attribution and legally protected descriptive uses are not prohibited by this policy. No trademark registration is asserted.

For a planned use of the project's distinctive branding that may imply official endorsement, request permission through the official repository's issues.

The bundled PNG icon and mascot are currently distributed under Apache-2.0 as described above. This copyright permission cannot be withdrawn retroactively for compliant copies. The separate trademark provision concerns misleading uses as branding and does not prohibit distribution of open-source images under their applicable copyright license. If the publisher later wants different copyright terms for future artwork, that requires a separately reviewed asset-licensing decision and compatibility assessment.

The RRH mascot and icons were generated for the project with AI assistance. The exact scope of protectable rights in the artwork and any third-party conflicts depends on applicable law.
